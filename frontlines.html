FRONTLINES

Conquer the world. Build cities, harbors and fleets.
How to play
  • Pick a start: click any land during the countdown.
  • Left click neutral land or an enemy to attack with the Attack size share of your troops. If you can't reach them by land, a boat goes instead.
  • Right click (long-press on touch) for the action menu: build, send boats, alliances and nukes.
  • Troops vs workers: troops fight and defend, workers earn gold. Population grows toward a cap that rises with land and cities.
  • Buildings (keys 1–8): City +max population · Port: trade ships and warships · Defense Post: holds the line · Missile Silo: nukes · SAM: shoots down nukes.
  • Mountains and hills are harder to take. Hold 80% of the land to win.
  • Drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, WASD/arrows to move, Space to find home, P to pause.
